# Hero lab lessons

Concrete notes from building and measuring `/lab/composed`. Updated as rounds finish.

## From the research, applied before the first build

- A rotating reel that cannot be touched scores as a showpiece. Shuffle, chips, and the play mark are what make the hero playable.
- Tone.js and three.js do not belong in the first paint. Tone starts inside the play-tap handler. three.js waits until the card is near and the visitor asks.
- The share is the URL, not a post button. Copy and a local download are the only outputs.
- Physical work is credited with text links. The verified shops are Nervous System, Tyler Hobbs, and Generative Goods. Their images are not hotlinked.
